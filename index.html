About the property

Description property

Sherry Fitzgerald Draper is proud to present this substantial family home standing proud on C. 1.4 acres with large detached garage unit situated just 2 kms from the hospital, university, Sligo city & within walking distance from the popular local Sudbury school.

As you approach this impressive family home up a long shared driveway you get a sense of the scale of the mature private grounds cleverly planted with love & care by its present owner.

As you enter inside you are welcomed by a spacious porch & entrance hallway, cosy sitting room with bay window & feature open fireplace, formal dining room with bay window, feature open fireplace & a door leading to the kitchen, well equipped kitchen with extensive range of fitted units, second living room with solid fuel stove & a sliding door to the rear, handy utility room & separate wc.

On the first floor are five fine sized bedrooms (three with ensuites) a main house bathroom & large landing. The attic is easily accessed from the landing via a stira stairs and is well floored out and it has a large skylight letting in great natural light.To the rear of the residence is a spacious private paved patio that links well with the house and rear garden & a large detached garage unit that has the potential for many different uses including,workshop, studio or granny flat accommodation subject to planning. The unit is totally detached & independent from the main residence and has huge potential to be more than what it is currently.

The grounds of this impressive residence span to approximately 1.4 acres and are a truly special feature with beautifully planted mature trees & shrubberies providing a haven of peace and an oasis of calm which contribute to a sense of complete seclusion from the world yet only 2km from Sligo.

Viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate the convenient location & all that this home has to offer.
